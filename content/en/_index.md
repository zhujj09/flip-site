        ## Research areas

        FLIP stands for **F**low, **L**ift and **I**ntelligent **P**roduction. Based at China University of Petroleum (Beijing) and co-supervising students with Prof. Jianli Wang of Southeast University, the group studies:

        - **Multiphase flow** — steady and transient wellbore/pipeline models, PINN-based fast computation, liquid-loading prediction.
        - **Artificial lift** — electrical submersible pumps, plunger lift and hydraulic jet pumps: mechanisms, modeling, design and diagnosis.
        - **Intelligent production** — data-driven diagnosis and decision-making with field production data; MFL pipeline inspection.
