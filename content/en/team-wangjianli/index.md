### Biography

Jianli Wang works on measurement system development and testing, machine learning, and energy system optimization. He received his B.S. from Xi'an Jiaotong University (2005) and Ph.D. in Power Engineering and Engineering Thermophysics from Tsinghua University (2010), and then joined the School of Mechanical Engineering, Southeast University. He was a visiting scholar at Caltech (2012–2013) and Purdue University (2013–2014). He has led four NSFC projects and industry collaborations with Sinopec, Dongfang Electric and others.

### Research interests

Measurement system development and testing; intelligent analysis and inspection; design and optimization of energy-saving systems.
