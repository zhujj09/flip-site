### Biography

Jianjun Zhu works on multiphase flow modeling, artificial-lift system design and optimization, and oil and gas production data mining, including steady/transient multiphase flow, artificial-lift equipment and processes (ESP, plunger lift, jet pump, gas lift), and production big data and intelligent diagnosis. He was listed in the Elsevier–Stanford World's Top 2% Scientists (single year, 2025–2026). He has led NSFC Young Scientists and General projects, a sub-task of a National Science and Technology Major Project, and more than 15 industry projects with CNPC, Sinopec and PipeChina. He has published over 100 papers (50+ SCI) with more than 3,500 Google Scholar citations.

### Education and experience

- 2019–present, China University of Petroleum (Beijing)
- 2017–2019, Tulsa University Artificial Lift Projects (TUALP), Research Associate
- 2012–2017, Ph.D. in Petroleum Engineering, The University of Tulsa
- 2009–2012, M.S. in Power Engineering and Engineering Thermophysics, Tsinghua University
- 2005–2009, B.S. in Thermal Energy and Power Engineering, University of Science and Technology Beijing

### Honors and service

- First Prize, Science and Technology Progress Award, Chinese Nuclear Society (2025)
- Outstanding Reviewer, SPE Production & Operations (2022–2024) and SPE Journal (2025)
- Honorary Young Editorial Board Member, Petroleum Science (appointed 2020; honorary 2025)
- Committee member for SPE ATCE and several SPE international awards
