FLIP's research covers flow, lift and production data of oil and gas wells from the bottom hole to the surface, in six areas. Experiments, numerical simulation (CFD, OLGA), mechanistic modeling and data-driven methods are combined and cross-validated. Full publication list: [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=sfsM2TUAAAAJ&hl=en).
